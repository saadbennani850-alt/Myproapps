Privacy Policy — Endoscope Camera
Effective Date: February 27, 2026
Last Updated: February 27, 2026

1. Overview
Endoscope Camera (the "app") is an Android application that lets users connect and view live footage from USB endoscope and borescope cameras, capture photos, and record videos directly to their device.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect
We do not collect, store, or transmit:

Your name, email address, or any contact details
Location data (GPS or network-based)
Device identifiers for tracking purposes
Browsing, usage, or behavioral analytics
Any data from the camera feed
All camera footage remains entirely on your device.

3. Permissions Used
The app requests the following Android permissions, used solely to enable core functionality:

Permission	Purpose
CAMERA	Required to access USB camera devices
USB_DEVICE	Connects to USB endoscope/borescope hardware
READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE	Reads saved photos/videos for the gallery
WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE	Saves captured photos and recorded videos
RECORD_AUDIO	Optional audio recording during video capture
FOREGROUND_SERVICE	Runs background camera recording service
POST_NOTIFICATIONS	Shows background recording status notification
No permission data is shared with third parties.

4. Data Storage
Photos and videos captured through the app are saved directly to your device's local storage (DCIM/EndoscopeCamera folder). No data is uploaded to any server or cloud service.

5. Third-Party Advertising (AdMob)
This app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use certain device and usage data to serve relevant ads, in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

You can opt out of personalized ads by adjusting your device settings under Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.

6. Children's Privacy
This app is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be reflected by an updated "Last Updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
