Privacy Policy for Dog Whistle App



Dog Whistle (“the App”) is provided as a free application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our app.

Information Collection and Use

The Dog Whistle app does not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information.

The app may use your device’s speaker and audio system to generate ultrasonic sound frequencies intended for dog training purposes. No audio is recorded, stored, or transmitted.

Permissions

The app may request the following permissions:

Audio/Speaker access – to emit sound frequencies.

Internet access (if applicable) – only for displaying ads or app updates.

These permissions are used strictly for app functionality.

Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services such as:

Google AdMob

Google Play Services

These services may collect information used to identify your device for advertising and analytics purposes. Their data handling is governed by their own privacy policies:

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data Security

We value your trust. Since the app does not collect personal data, there is no personal information stored or shared. Reasonable security measures are in place to protect app functionality.

Children’s Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page. You are advised to review this policy periodically.
