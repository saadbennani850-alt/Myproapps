Privacy Policy for Reminder Pro

Reminder Pro respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our Android application.

1. Information We Collect

Reminder Pro does not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information.

The app may access the following device features only to function properly:

Notifications (to remind you of scheduled tasks)

Local storage (to save reminders on your device)

All reminder data is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to any external servers.

2. How We Use Information

Any data used by the app is strictly for:

Creating and managing reminders

Triggering scheduled notifications

We do not use your data for analytics, tracking, advertising, or profiling.

3. Data Sharing

We do not share your data with third parties.
Reminder Pro does not integrate with ad networks, analytics services, or external APIs.

4. Data Security

Your data remains on your device. While we take reasonable measures to ensure the app functions securely, you are responsible for protecting access to your device.

5. Children’s Privacy

Reminder Pro is safe for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date
